BUDGET HAND

3+ players per table (6–7 works well) · 1 standard deck per table, jokers out · 15–20 min
Card value = number × ₹100 · J=1,100 · Q=1,200 · K=1,300 · A=100

What each suit means

♥Hearts — IncomeAdd the card's value to Wallet. Move 10% of it straight to the Emergency Fund — always.
♣Clubs — NeedsMust pay from Wallet. Can't cover it? Pay from Fund. Fund empty too? Mark one Debt.
♦Diamonds — Wants10 seconds: Pay (value moves Wallet → Joy) or Skip (nothing happens).
♠Spades — EmergencyPay from Fund only. Fund can't cover it? Shortfall × 1.5 becomes Debt.

How to play

  1. One player draws the top card and reads its suit aloud.
  2. The table resolves it by the suit rule — fast, except Diamonds, which get a real 10-second discussion.
  3. Update your three running totals: Wallet, Emergency Fund, Joy — plus Debt if it comes to that.
  4. Keep drawing until the deck runs out, or your facilitator calls time (~20–25 cards is a good quick round).
FINAL SCORE = WALLET + EMERGENCY FUND + JOY − DEBT

Facilitator tip: don't referee Clubs/Spades — the rule is automatic. Only step in if a Diamonds call stalls a table.
